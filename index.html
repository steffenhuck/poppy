Find the Treat

Treats are hidden in the garden. Poppy can't see them, but she can smell them.

Tap a spot. Poppy runs there and sniffs, and the spot takes the colour of her nose: blue for cold, yellow for faint and warm, orange for warmer, red for hot: the treat is on one of the eight squares touching hers. Find every treat before she gets tired!

How Poppy's nose works

The same rule on every level. Around a treat, the garden smells like this:

hotwarmerwarmfaintcold

Red (hot): the treat is on one of the 8 squares touching Poppy's, diagonals included. Orange (warmer): two squares away. Yellow (warm): three. Pale yellow (faint): four. Blue (cold): nothing within four squares. Distances are as the crow flies, so the bands are rings, not boxes.

  • Every sniff is one tap. The colour stays on the square, so you can think.
  • With more than one treat, Poppy's nose goes by the nearest one.
  • Colours never change. Each one shows the nearest treat at the time of the sniff. So once Poppy has eaten a treat, a colour near it may have been about that one, about another treat, or both.
  • Tapping the square with the treat finds it.
